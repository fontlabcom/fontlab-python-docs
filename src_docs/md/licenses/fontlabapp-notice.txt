Font Info panel provenance
==========================

src/fontlabapp/_font_info_dialog.py adapts the shared dialog shell from the six
Font Info scripts in fontlab-samples (fontlab-python-examples archive,
_old/part4/fontlab-samples/samples/fontinfo_*.py), licensed under the Apache
