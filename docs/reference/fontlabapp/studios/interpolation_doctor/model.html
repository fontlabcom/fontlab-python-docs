    fontlabapp.studios.interpolation_doctor.model

    model

    Interpolation Doctor findings: compatibility, counts, literal reports and the blend.

    What: pure readers over explicit FontLab layers (flGlyph.findLayer or flGlyph.layers; never fgGlyph) that rebuild the findings of ten Scriptmix scripts: Masters > Compatibility Check (signature/check), Anchors > Compatibility Report (anchor_sets), Components > Compatibility (component_lists/odd_masters), Contours > Node Statistics (layer_counts/odd_cells), Reports > Master Node Compatibility Report (node_report), Reports > Why You Not Compatible (why_report), Reports > Point Counter (point_report), Reports > Point Counter (Master Layers) (master_point_report), Masters > Interpolation Preview (outline/problem/blend) and Contours > Make Compatible (compatible_plan). Report text is the originals' literal output. Why: studios may not import fontlabapp.masters/contours, so the small readers are restated here with the same counter definitions.
