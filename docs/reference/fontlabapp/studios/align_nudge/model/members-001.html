    align_changes 

    align_changes(layer: Layer, mode: str, all_nodes: bool = False, coordinate: float | None = None) -> list[Change]

    Align And Distribute plan_layer: align to the bounds or a coordinate, or distribute nodes.

    Whole selected contours move as units, partial contours contribute selected nodes with their unselected handles, components move by their transform. Distribution sorts by the axis (ties keep contour/node order), keeps both endpoints and needs three or more items. Anchors never move.

    overlap_changes 

    overlap_changes(layer: Layer, angle: float) -> list[Change]

    Overlap Nodes: move each selected tip's adjacent corner onto it and align the next handle.

    The next corner is tried first, then the previous one. The corner becomes sharp; the handle x follows the master's italic angle (FontLab sign, negated), truncated to an integer in glyph space; handle y stays.
