    fontlabapp.studios.anchor_studio.model

    model

    Anchor Studio maths: Qt-free planners, safety checks and the read-back check.

    What: A layer's anchors are read once into Pin tuples (name, x, y). Every planner turns those pins (plus the layer's ink box and advance) into Change rows; a Change with an edit is written, one with a problem is shown and refused. Edits are ("set", name, (x, y)) (add the anchor or move the only anchor of that name), ("remove", index) and ("rename", index, new) where index is the pin's place in the snapshot. verify compares the stored anchors with the planned result within TOLERANCE and names every sub-unit move that FontLab rounded to no change (Scriptmix TODO:78), so the studio reports it instead of claiming success. Why: The planners reproduce the Scriptmix Scripts/Anchors tools; keeping them free of Qt and FontLab makes every rule unit-testable. Used by fontlabapp.studios.anchor_studio.session and .tabs.
